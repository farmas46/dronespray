DroneSpray pre-mixing systems for agricultural drones (English)

DroneSpray units mix and refill spray solution for agricultural spraying drones in the field: polyethylene tanks from 110 to 600 litres, a 1 HP stainless centrifugal pump with up to 80 L/min flow (12 V diaphragm pump on the DS110), continuous dual agitation and container rinsing. CE and UKCA certified. Made by FARMA Kyratsas Agricultural Machinery in Larissa, Greece, and exported across Europe. The website is available in Greek, English, German, Spanish, Italian and French.
